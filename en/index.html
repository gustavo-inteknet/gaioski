01 About

About

Monitoring gives you the warning. But a warning only becomes protection when someone decides to act.
from a real case, 2026

I started in 2015 as a support intern, fixing printers and creating Active Directory accounts. Eleven years later I'm still at the same company, IntekNet, now as CCO. Along the way I worked through servers, networks, firewalls and Microsoft 365, and more recently through leading the team that looks after all of it.

If that path taught me anything, it's that almost no IT problem is purely technical. The file that won't open from OneDrive is an information architecture problem. The backup that fails to restore is a missing second layer. The new hire who spends day one waiting for access is a process problem. I like finding the cause before swapping the part.

Today I split my time between operations, clients, and something I'm genuinely excited about: using AI and automation to take manual work off the team's plate and give better numbers to the people making decisions.

04 Field notes

Real stories, no client names.

Continuity01

The backup that failed when it mattered

Monitoring flagged a failing disk. The replacement got postponed, the disk died, and the backup, tested every month, failed to restore. What saved the day was a second backup, in a different tool.

One backup is a bet. Redundancy means layers that don't depend on each other.

Microsoft 36502

The 255-character limit

The file opens in the browser but not on the PC. In the cloud a path can be up to 400 characters; synced to Windows, the ceiling drops to 255. A migration full of nested folders blows past that easily.

It isn't Microsoft 365. It's information architecture.

read on LinkedIn (PT)

Infrastructure03

The workaround that became the foundation

The 2019 script that still runs billing. The spreadsheet that only opens on one computer. The old PC under a desk acting as a server. When we take over an environment, we hunt for these pieces before they break.

Improvising under pressure has value. The problem is when "for now" becomes the foundation.

read on LinkedIn (PT)

Management04

Silence isn't health

Plenty of companies judge IT by the absence of complaints. But years without an incident can be luck, not management. The real signals are different: a warning before users notice, a clear owner, a backup someone can prove works.

Reactive works right up until the day it doesn't.
